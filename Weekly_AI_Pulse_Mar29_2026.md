# Weekly AI Pulse: March 22–29, 2026

### Summary
This week marked a definitive "Hardware Inflection" in the AI industry, as major players pivoted from model-only competition to multi-billion dollar vertical integration of custom silicon and agentic infrastructure. The landscape also saw a strategic consolidation of generative video efforts and a surge in high-stakes legal battles over AI safety and sovereignty.

### Highlights

*   **Arm AGI CPU: The Silicon Foundation for Agentic AI**
    *   **Impact:** Arm launched its first in-house designed "AGI CPU," specifically optimized for inference in agentic AI workloads, with Meta as its debut customer.
    *   **Context:** This move transitions Arm from a licensing model to a direct hardware provider, addressing the massive compute requirements of autonomous agents that require low-latency, rack-scale performance.

*   **Elon Musk’s $25B "Terafab" Announcement**
    *   **Impact:** A collaborative venture between Tesla, SpaceX, and xAI to build a $25 billion chip manufacturing complex in Texas aiming for 1 terawatt of AI compute annually.
    *   **Context:** By declaring "war on TSMC," Musk is seeking total supply chain sovereignty to power future generations of Grok and Tesla's Optimus robots without reliance on external foundries.

*   **Meta TRIBE v2: Trimodal Brain Encoding at Scale**
    *   **Impact:** Meta FAIR released TRIBE v2, a foundation model capable of predicting human brain activity across 70,000 voxels based on video, audio, and speech inputs.
    *   **Context:** This breakthrough allows for the simulation of brain reactions without expensive fMRI scans, opening new frontiers in neural interface research and personalized AI interactions.

*   **The "Claude Mythos" Leak and the Capybara Tier**
    *   **Impact:** A data leak revealed Anthropic’s "Mythos" model, part of a new "Capybara" tier that reportedly outperforms Claude 3.5 Opus in coding, reasoning, and offensive cybersecurity.
    *   **Context:** Mythos represents a significant leap in agentic capabilities, prompting Anthropic to prioritize defensive releases to harden global infrastructure against AI-driven exploits.

*   **OpenAI Shuts Down Sora and "Adult Mode" Projects**
    *   **Impact:** OpenAI abruptly discontinued its Sora video-generation app and indefinitely paused "Adult Mode" research to consolidate compute resources.
    *   **Context:** This strategic retreat signals the end of the generative video hype cycle as OpenAI redirects its massive GPU clusters toward core reasoning models and the "Strawberry" reasoning architecture.

*   **Apple’s iOS 27 "AI Storefront" Pivot**
    *   **Impact:** Reports indicate Apple will open Siri to rival LLMs like Gemini and Claude through an Extensions system, ending ChatGPT’s exclusive integration.
    *   **Context:** Apple is positioning itself as the primary AI distribution layer (the "Storefront"), leveraging its 1.2 billion iPhone user base to collect commissions on AI subscriptions rather than competing solely on model performance.

### Conclusion
The trend this week is the **normalization of AI infrastructure**. We are moving past the era of "model-as-a-service" into an era of **"verticalized AI stacks,"** where the most successful companies are those that control the entire pipeline from custom silicon (Arm AGI, Terafab) to the distribution interface (Apple’s AI Storefront). The shutdown of Sora further suggests that the industry is prioritizing **utility and reasoning** over pure creative generation, focusing on tools that can perform complex, multi-step actions in the real world.
